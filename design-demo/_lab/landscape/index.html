Horizon

Once the head has scrolled away, the ridges sink to a low, quiet band along the bottom of the view and stay there while you read. No cloud sea, no fade to blank paper.

The band keeps its crests in the bottom quarter of the view (a fifth on a phone), so nothing but mist sits behind the reading line.

Reading

Each block of text carries a veil: mist banks up behind it, so small print never sits on bare mountain. Up to eight veils are painted at once.

The mist rests while you read; it drifts again in the head, in a vista and in the closing.

After the vista

The vista lifted the horizon back to the full scene while it crossed the middle of the view; the pointer's wake is strongest there.

Closing
