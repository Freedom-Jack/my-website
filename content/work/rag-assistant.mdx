## Context

Insurance support teams spend much of their day on routine questions about policies and claims. The goal was an assistant that answers them from the insurer’s own knowledge base, in several languages, around the clock.

## What I did

I architected and shipped the system: retrieval-augmented generation over insurance knowledge bases, built with LangChain across Azure, AWS and GCP.

<Note term="RAG">Retrieval-augmented generation. The model answers from passages retrieved from a knowledge base, not from its training alone.</Note>

<Note term="LangChain">An open-source framework for building applications on language models.</Note>

## Outcome

About **90%** of questions resolved without a person, across a user base of **50,000+**. **525%** return on investment within 12 months. CGI Impact Award 2024.
