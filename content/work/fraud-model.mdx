## Context

Unrecovered fraud shows up directly in the loss ratio. The benchmark to beat was a model from a top-five bank.

## What I did

I built and shipped a gradient-boosting plus logistic-regression model, with batch scoring in a Python and T-SQL pipeline.

## Outcome

**321%** lift in recovered fraud losses versus the top-five bank benchmark.

Client work, no public page.
